About the project

A vault for everything
you learn as you build.

We learn the same things twice. A clever pattern, a tricky bit of syntax, the name of the person who invented it — gone by the time we need it again. TechVault is your second memory as a developer: catalog the technologies you use, the people who created them and the features worth keeping, so the next time you forget how something works, it's one search away.
